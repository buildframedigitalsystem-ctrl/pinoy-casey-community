# pinoy-casey-community

Pinoy Casey Community, Inc. (PCCI) is a newly established nonprofit organization run by volunteers in the City of Casey located southeast of Melbourne. PCCI was born from the collaboration of Filipinos across various networks in Casey, recognizing the necessity for a dedicated organization to foster camaraderie among Filipinos residing in the area. We identified the need to establish initiatives aimed at the well-being of elderly Filipinos, empowering and guiding Filipino youth, facilitating family bonding experiences, particularly for new migrants and families settling in the region. Our mission extends to promoting Filipino culture, including traditional dances, music, and cuisine, while also advocating for sports and social activities to engage the growing Filipino community.

By establishing a new community group, we aim to introduce additional events and activities within Casey, fostering a stronger sense of community and belongingness among residents.
