About The Organization

Built for Filipino-Australians in the City of Casey

Pinoy Casey Community Inc. is a community-focused organization created to bring Filipino-Australians together through cultural activities, support programs, events, and meaningful connections.

This platform helps the organization present its mission publicly while also giving the admin team a backend system to manage records, updates, and activities.
