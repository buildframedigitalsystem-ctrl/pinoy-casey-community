About The Organization

Who We Are

Pinoy Casey Community Inc. supports Filipino-led and multicultural communities through programs, events, leadership initiatives, and partnerships.

Our mission is to create stronger community connection, inclusion, economic participation, and opportunities for individuals and families.

Governance, policies, child safety, and community wellbeing remain central to the organization.
