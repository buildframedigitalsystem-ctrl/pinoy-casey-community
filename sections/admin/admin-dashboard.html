Welcome to Pinoy Casey Admin Dashboard

This backend system is designed to support the long-term Pinoy Casey Community Inc. platform using Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, and GitHub Pages.


Admins can manage:

  • Participants and community registrations
  • Volunteers and activity helpers
  • Partners, sponsors, donors, and funding bodies
  • VIP, media, stallholders, activities, and entertainment records
  • Event requests, tickets, stallholder bookings, and payment records
  • Announcements, updates, and community resources
  • Gallery albums organized by project, activity, and year
  • Messages, inquiries, and contact records
  • Reports for board review, impact tracking, and future funding support

Platform Flow

The public website collects registrations, event requests, inquiries, payments, and support records. The admin dashboard helps the organization review, organize, and manage the data.

  • Website visitor submits a form
  • Google Apps Script receives the request
  • Google Sheets stores the record
  • Admin team reviews and manages the information
  • Board can use the records for planning, reports, and impact tracking
